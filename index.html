FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the most common questions about our home healthcare services in Bangalore.

What home healthcare services does ARENEO offer in Bangalore?

ARENEO Healthcare offers a comprehensive range of home healthcare services including nursing care at home, patient attendant services, physiotherapy at home, medical assistance (IV therapy, wound dressing, injections), newborn and mother care, and elderly care across Bangalore.

Are your nurses and caregivers certified and background verified?

Yes, absolutely. All our nurses, patient attendants, and healthcare professionals are certified, trained, and undergo thorough background verification before being assigned to any patient. Your safety and trust are our top priorities.

How quickly can you arrange a caregiver at home?

We strive to arrange a caregiver within 24 hours of your request. For urgent requirements, we do our best to provide same-day service. Please call or WhatsApp us at +91 94004 60136 for urgent bookings.

What areas in Bangalore do you serve?

We provide home healthcare services across Bangalore including areas such as Whitefield, Koramangala, Indiranagar, HSR Layout, JP Nagar, Jayanagar, Marathahalli, Electronic City, Yelahanka, and more. Contact us to confirm availability in your specific area.

How much do your home healthcare services cost?

Our pricing is transparent and competitive. Costs vary depending on the type of service, duration, and specific requirements. We offer flexible packages for hourly, daily, and monthly engagements. Contact us for a free consultation and customized quote tailored to your needs.

Can I book a service for a senior citizen living alone?

Yes! We specialize in elderly care and can assign a trained caregiver or companion for senior citizens. Our caregivers assist with daily activities, medication reminders, meal preparation, mobility assistance, and provide companionship to ensure the safety and well-being of your loved ones.
