Who We Are

ARENEO Healthcare Solution and Studies LLP

ARENEO Healthcare Solution and Studies LLP is a registered healthcare service company based in Bangalore, India. We are dedicated to transforming the way families access professional medical care — by bringing qualified healthcare professionals directly to patients' homes.

Founded with a deep commitment to compassion and excellence, ARENEO bridges the gap between clinical healthcare and the comfort of home. We understand that recovering at home, surrounded by loved ones, leads to better outcomes — and we make that possible by ensuring the same quality of care you'd expect in a hospital setting.

Our team comprises certified nurses, trained patient attendants, licensed physiotherapists, and experienced care coordinators who work together to create personalized care plans for every patient — whether they are recovering from surgery, managing a chronic condition, or simply need assistance in their daily lives.
