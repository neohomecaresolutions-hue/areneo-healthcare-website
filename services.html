Home Care

Home Care Services in Bangalore

Our home care services are designed to support individuals who need assistance with daily activities but prefer to remain in the comfort of their own home. Our trained caregivers provide respectful, dignified support tailored to each individual's needs and routine.

Ideal for elderly individuals, patients recovering from illness or surgery, and anyone requiring long-term support, our home care service ensures your loved one is safe, comfortable, and well-cared for at all times.

  • Personal hygiene assistance (bathing, grooming, dressing)
  • Meal preparation and feeding assistance
  • Mobility support and fall prevention
  • Medication reminders and management
  • Companionship and emotional support
  • Light housekeeping and home organization
  • Doctor visit accompaniment

Nursing Care

Professional Nursing Care at Home

Our certified nursing professionals bring hospital-quality clinical care to your doorstep. Whether you need post-operative care, chronic disease management, or specialized nursing procedures, our nurses are equipped to handle it all with the highest standards of clinical excellence.

All our nurses are BSc / GNM qualified, registered with the Nursing Council, and undergo regular training to stay current with best practices in healthcare.

  • Post-surgical nursing care and wound dressing
  • Intravenous (IV) therapy and drip administration
  • Injection administration (IM, IV, SC)
  • Vital signs monitoring (BP, pulse, temperature, SpO2)
  • Urinary catheter insertion and care
  • Bed sore prevention and wound care
  • Diabetic care and insulin management

Patient Care

Patient Attendant Services

Our trained patient attendants provide dedicated, round-the-clock support for patients in hospitals, nursing homes, or at home. They act as an extension of the family — ensuring your loved one is never alone, always comfortable, and receiving the attentive care they deserve.

Our attendants are trained in basic patient care, infection prevention, and emergency response, ensuring both safety and comfort at all times.

  • 24/7 patient supervision and monitoring
  • Assistance with bathing, feeding, and toileting
  • Turning and positioning for bedridden patients
  • Hospital patient attendant services
  • Post-ICU recovery support
  • Elderly care and dementia support
  • Emotional support and companionship
