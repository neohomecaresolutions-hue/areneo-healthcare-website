FAQ

Common Questions Before Booking

Here are some of the most common questions patients and families ask when contacting us for the first time.

How do I book a home healthcare service with ARENEO?

You can book a service by calling us at +91 94004 60136 or +91 94464 60136, sending a WhatsApp message, filling out the contact form on this page, or emailing us at info@neohomecaresolution.com. Our care coordinator will get back to you promptly to discuss your needs and finalize the arrangement.

Is there a consultation fee for initial enquiries?

No, there is absolutely no charge for initial consultations. We believe in understanding your needs first before recommending the right service. Contact us for a completely free consultation and customized quote.

How soon can a caregiver be arranged after booking?

We strive to arrange a caregiver within 24 hours of your booking. For urgent requirements, we do our best to provide same-day service depending on availability. Please call or WhatsApp us directly for urgent cases.

Can I change or cancel a booked service?

Yes, we understand that healthcare needs can change. Please contact us as early as possible to modify or cancel your booking. We will work with you to accommodate any changes with minimal disruption.

Do you offer services on weekends and public holidays?

Yes, we are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — including weekends and public holidays. Healthcare doesn't take a day off, and neither do we. Our team is always ready to support you whenever you need us.
